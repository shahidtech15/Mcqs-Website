    Critical Legal Boundary Distinctions

    Examiners intentionally design distractors around these classic statutory distinctions. Memorize these key thresholds:

    Offence / Provision A Offence / Provision B Decisive Legal Distinction
    Section 34 PPC
    (Common Intention)    		 Section 149 PPC
    (Common Object)    		 Number & Agreement: Sec 149 requires an unlawful assembly of at least 5 persons sharing a common object. Sec 34 applies to 2 or more persons acting in furtherance of a pre-arranged common intention.
    Section 378 PPC
    (Theft)    		 Section 383 PPC
    (Extortion)    		 Delivery & Fear: In Theft, property is taken without consent by moving it secretly. In Extortion, property is delivered by the victim under fear of injury.
    Section 390 PPC
    (Robbery)    		 Section 391 PPC
    (Dacoity)    		 Conjoint Threshold: Robbery becomes Dacoity ONLY when committed or attempted by 5 or more persons acting conjointly.
    Section 302(a)/(b) PPC
    (Qisas / Tazir Death/Life)    		 Section 302(c) PPC
    (Grave & Sudden Provocation)    		 Mitigation: Sec 302(c) applies when intentional killing occurs without premeditation under the sudden loss of self-control caused by grave provocation (max 25 years imprisonment).
    Section 167 CrPC
    (Police Physical Remand)    		 Section 344 CrPC
    (Judicial Remand)    		 Absolute 14-Day Cap: Police custody for interrogation under Sec 167 cannot exceed 14 days in aggregate. Further detentions must be in judicial lockup under Sec 344.
    Article 62(1)(f)
    (Disqualification)    		 Article 63A
    (Party Defection)    		 Current Rulings: Art 62(1)(f) disqualification is now capped at 5 years (Supreme Court Jan 2024). Defecting votes under Art 63A must be counted, followed by de-seating (SC Review Oct 2024).
    Article 46 QSO
    (Dying Declaration)    		 Hearsay Exclusion
    (General Rule)    		 Nemo Moriturus: A statement by a dying person as to the cause of death is admissible without cross-examination because a person on the threshold of death will not face their Maker with a lie.
