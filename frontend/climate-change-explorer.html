Glaciers, Polar Ice Sheets & The Third Pole

ICIMOD • NASA Cryosphere
Arctic Amplification

Arctic Sea Ice Retreat

September minimum sea ice extent is declining at -12.6% per decade. The ice-albedo feedback causes dark open ocean water to absorb 94% of solar energy, warming the Arctic 3 to 4 times faster than the global average.

Ice Sheet Mass Loss

Greenland & Antarctica

Greenland loses ~270 Gt of ice per year; Antarctica loses ~150 Gt/year. West Antarctica's Thwaites "Doomsday Glacier" alone holds enough ice to raise sea level by 65 cm and destabilize the ice sheet.

The Third Pole: Pakistan's Glacial Water Towers

Pakistan holds over 7,253 glaciers—the largest concentration of glacial ice anywhere on Earth outside the polar ice caps. Between 70% and 85% of the Indus River's flow is sustained by snowpack and glacial melt.

Pakistan's Climate Vulnerability & High-Yield CSS Notes

Germanwatch CRI • PDNA 2022

The Climate Injustice Paradox

Pakistan emits less than 0.9% of global greenhouse gases, yet consistently ranks among the top 10 most climate-vulnerable countries in the world on the Germanwatch Global Climate Risk Index (CRI). It faces simultaneous risks: melting glaciers in the north, extreme desert heatwaves (>50°C in Jacobabad/Nawabshah), catastrophic monsoon floods, and sea intrusion along the Sindh coast.
