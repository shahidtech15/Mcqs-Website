How to Study Newspaper Editorials for CSS, PMS & FPSC Examinations

Consistent editorial reading is the cornerstone of high-scoring answers in CSS English Essay, English Précis & Composition, Current Affairs, and Pakistan Affairs. Rather than memorizing news headlines, successful candidates dissect each editorial into five exam-ready components: (1) the underlying historical or constitutional context, (2) the author's central thesis statement, (3) empirical evidence sourced from official reports such as the Pakistan Economic Survey, State Bank of Pakistan, IMF, or United Nations, (4) academic vocabulary in context, and (5) balanced policy recommendations for 20-mark analytical questions.
