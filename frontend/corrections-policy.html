Corrections Policy & Error Reporting Procedure

Last Updated: November 2026 | Transparent Verification & Prompt Remediation
Accuracy Matters in Competitive Exams: A single miskeyed MCQ or outdated statutory reference can affect a candidate's preparation. We welcome and prioritize correction reports from students, teachers, and subject specialists.

1. What You Can Report

Users are encouraged to report any of the following issues across our 74+ pages, interactive tools, and 15,000+ MCQ bank:

  • Incorrect MCQ Answer Keys: Questions where the marked correct option or explanation conflicts with an authoritative textbook, bare act, or official key.
  • Outdated Statutory or Constitutional Provisions: Legal sections or constitutional articles that have been amended or repealed by subsequent legislation.
  • Factual or Statistical Errors: Historical dates, geographical coordinates, scientific constants, or economic figures requiring correction.
  • Technical Issues & Broken Links: Non-functioning interactive controls, display issues on mobile devices, or broken external reference links.
  • Copyright or Attribution Concerns: Material believed to require updated attribution or removal under our Copyright Policy.

2. How to Submit a Correction Report

You can report an error through either of these channels:

  1. Via the Contact & Error Reporting Form or Direct Email: Visit our Contact Us & Error Reporting Page or email shahidali.shahid@gmail.com, select "Report an MCQ Error / Content Correction", and include the page URL, question text (or ID), and the authoritative source supporting the correction.
  2. Direct Question Feedback: Use the comment/feedback box beneath individual questions on the homepage or note the Question ID during a mock quiz review.

3. How Corrections Are Reviewed and Implemented

  • Step 1 — Logging: Every reported error is logged in our Administrative Review Queue.
  • Step 2 — Primary Source Verification: Our editorial team verifies the report against the relevant bare act (e.g., Constitution of Pakistan, PPC, CrPC, FIA Act), official Economic Survey, or standard academic reference.
  • Step 3 — Update & Explanation Note: Once verified, the answer key, explanation, or article text is corrected across both the primary dataset and offline fallback cache. Significant updates to articles include a visible "Last Updated" notice.
