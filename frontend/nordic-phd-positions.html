🇨🇭 WIPO Fellowship Program 2027 — Geneva, Switzerland (UN Headquarters)

CHF 5,000 / Month (~$5,650 / Mo Tax-Exempt) + Flights & Medical

The World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) — a specialized agency of the United Nations headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland — offers fully-funded 12 to 24-month fellowships for Natural Science (Chemistry, Biotechnology, Pharmaceuticals, Physics, Green/Climate Tech), Engineering, AI/IT, Economics, and Legal graduates from developing countries (including Pakistan).

Flagship 24-Month Cohort • Age ≤ 35

1. WIPO Young Experts Program (YEP 2027)

2-year full-time fellowship at WIPO Geneva HQ mentoring future science, innovation, and IP leaders from developing nations. Pays CHF 5,000/month, 75% medical insurance, accident cover, and round-trip flights. Requires a university degree (BS/MS/PhD) + 2 years experience.

12–36 Months • Natural Sciences & STEM

2. WIPO GREEN, Re:Search & PCT Science Fellowships

Ideal for Chemistry, Biotech, Environmental Science, Physics, and Engineering MS/PhD holders working on climate-change clean technology transfer (WIPO GREEN), global health/biotech R&D partnerships (WIPO Re:Search), patent landscape analytics, and scientific terminology.

12–36 Months • Data, Economics & Law

3. Global Innovation Index (GII) & Legal Fellowships

Recruits quantitative researchers, data scientists, economists, and legal fellows to co-author the flagship Global Innovation Index, manage Madrid/Hague international registrations, and support the WIPO Arbitration and Mediation Center.

Key Eligibility & Application Tip: No GRE and no application fee. Apply directly on WIPO's Taleo e-Recruitment portal when cohort calls open (Q3/Q4 for YEP; rolling year-round for technical/scientific fellowships).

How to Win a Salaried Natural Science PhD in the Nordics

1. Apply to Specific Project Calls (Not Generic Admissions)

In Sweden, Norway, and Denmark, ~90% of Natural Science PhDs are funded project vacancies posted by a Principal Investigator (PI) who won a research grant. Treat your application like a scientific job application tailored directly to the advertised project description.

2. Master's Thesis (30–60 ECTS) is Your #1 Asset

Nordic selection committees evaluate your independent research capability first. Always upload your full Master's thesis PDF (or a 2-page executive summary + draft chapters if defending soon) along with your experimental or computational methods list.

3. Only Have a 4-Year BS (16 Years)? Use Denmark's 4+4 / 3+5 Track!

While Sweden and Norway strictly require a completed 18-year Master's (240–300 ECTS), Aarhus University (GSNS) and University of Copenhagen (SCIENCE) in Denmark offer integrated 4-year and 5-year PhD tracks for top 4-year Bachelor's graduates.

4. No GRE Required & Flexible English Waivers

None of the 5 Nordic countries, Italy, or UNIGE/ESKAS in Switzerland require the GRE for Natural Science PhDs. Furthermore, many departments waive IELTS/TOEFL if your previous MS/BS degree was officially taught in English (MOI) and your technical interview demonstrates fluency.
