Sindh Police Mega Recruitment 2026:
2,000 ASI Investigation & 15,500 Constables

The Home Department, Government of Sindh has formally received a comprehensive recruitment proposal from the Office of the Inspector General of Police (IGP), Sindh. The sanctioned recruitment includes 2,000 Assistant Sub-Inspectors (Investigation) via SPSC, 5,000 Border Security Constables, and 10,500 General Police Constables via Sukkur IBA (STS) and the Sindh Job Portal (SJP).

Home Department Sindh has received a proposal for the recruitment of various posts:

  1. ASI Investigation: 2,000 Posts
  2. Border Security Constables: 5,000 Posts
  3. Police Constables: 10,500 Posts

These posts will be advertised through SPSC, SJP, and IBA.

Authority: CPO Sindh, Karachi • Letter No: AIGP/Admn/5454-90/2026 • Meeting Date: 29-10-2026 (Issued: 03-11-2026) • Chaired by: Worthy IGP Sindh

Verbatim Official Minutes Record Meeting held at Ibrahim Hall, 6th Floor, CPO Sindh, Karachi

“Item 8: Future Recruitment of Police Constables, Border Security Police and ASIs Investigation:
The proposals for future recruitment were discussed. A proposal for the recruitment of 2,000 ASIs Investigation has been sent to the Home Department for onward transmission to the Sindh Public Service Commission. A proposal for the recruitment of 5,000 Police Constables in the Border Security Police has been sent to the Government of Sindh, seeking relaxation of certain conditions. The Chair directed that the recruitment of Police Constables in the Border Security Police be pushed forward on priority, and that the new recruitment be expedited.
For general recruitment, 7,500 vacancies of Police Constable are presently available. As the process takes about one year, 3,000 anticipated vacancies have been added, bringing the total to 10,500.”

“Item 6: Recruitment in T&T Sindh and Creation of a Sindh Police Slot on the Sindh Job Portal:
The recruitment of 896 Head Constables (Wireless Operator) and 4,968 Driver Constables in T&T Sindh was discussed. The recruitment was advertised through the SIBA Testing Service; however, it is held up owing to... The Chair directed that the matter be pursued with the Services and Home Departments for the early creation of a separate Sindh Police slot on the Sindh Job Portal, so that the recruitment can commence and the shortage of staff in Telecommunication and MT can be addressed.”

“Item 9: Recruitment and Promotion against Vacancies of Junior Clerk:
The filling of 455 vacancies of Junior Clerk, which arose after the recent promotion of Junior Clerks to Senior Clerks, was discussed. It was agreed that, under the existing formula, 30% of the vacancies would be reserved for the promotion of Class-IV employees and the remaining vacancies would be filled through new recruitment. The Chair approved the reservation... and directed that the process for the remaining posts be initiated immediately after the recruitment of Police Constables and Border Security Police has been pushed forward.”

Official Source: Office of the Inspector General of Police, Sindh Karachi (Letter No. AIGP/Admn/5454-90/2026, Agenda Items 6, 8, 9 & 10, Pages 1–5 of 16).

How to Apply: Official Portals & Step-by-Step Process

1

Register Profile on Sindh Job Portal

Visit sindhjobs.gov.pk and create your National CNIC-based profile. Upload your matriculation certificate, domicile, PRC Form-D, and recent photographs.

2

Check SPSC Portal for ASI Investigation

For BPS-11 ASI posts, monitor spsc.gov.pk. Pay the official Challan of PKR 500 at the State Bank or National Bank of Pakistan prior to online submission.

3

Apply via Sukkur IBA (STS) Portal

For Constable & Border Security posts, log into apply.sts.net.pk. Generate the system challan and submit the candidate application form with accurate district allocation.
