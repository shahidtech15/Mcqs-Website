How to Play Scrabble

  • Place Tiles: Click a tile in your rack then click a square, or drag-and-drop tiles directly onto the 15×15 board. Click a placed (green-glow) tile to return it to your rack.
  • First Move: Must cover the center star (★) and be at least 2 letters long.
  • Connecting Words: Every subsequent move must lie in a single row or column and connect to at least one existing tile on the board.
  • Premium Multipliers: DL/TL multiply that tile's points; DW/TW multiply the entire word's score. Center ★ counts as a Double Word!
  • Bingo Bonus: Using all 7 tiles in one turn earns an automatic +50 bonus points!
  • Swap / Pass: Click Swap to select tiles to trade with the bag (requires 7+ tiles in bag). Two consecutive passes by both players end the game.
